# Code of conduct

This project is a professional space. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project channel) is expected to:

- be respectful and constructive, and assume good faith;
- critique ideas and code, never people;
- keep discussion on the topic of the project.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, sharing someone's private information without consent, and sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may restrict or remove anyone from the project's spaces, for behaviour that breaks this code.

Report concerns to **support@commertize.com** with the subject line "Conduct report: commertize-agents". Reports are handled confidentially.
